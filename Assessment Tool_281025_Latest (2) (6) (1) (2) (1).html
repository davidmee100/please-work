What the Tool Enables

1. Expert Assessments

Each expert receives their own copy of the tool to independently assess roles. They enter:

  • ratings
  • comments
  • recommended mitigation strategies (Build, Buy, Borrow, Bot, Retain)

After completing their review, they export a CSV file and return it.

2. Data Consolidation

All expert CSVs can be uploaded into the tool. The tool automatically:

  • averages capability and risk scores
  • merges comments
  • calculates overall role risk
  • produces a clean consolidated dataset for the workshop

3. Workshop Facilitation

In the workshop, the consolidated tool is projected so the team can:

  • review each role together
  • validate ratings
  • agree on mitigation actions
  • update the data live

You can export the final agreed version at the end of the session.

4. Follow‑Up Tracking

After the workshop, the same tool is used to monitor progress. You can:

  • update scores
  • adjust retention risks
  • refine or close mitigation actions
  • export updated data for follow‑up reviews (1 week, 1 month, 3 months)

Good Practice for Workshops

As we work through the data, please keep these points in mind:

  • Challenge each other. Ask questions, test assumptions, and make sure ratings are accurate.
  • Step 1: Review whether criticality, capabilities, and retention risk have been rated effectively.
  • Step 2: Discuss our current methods for addressing any gaps identified.
  • Stay open and constructive. The more we challenge, the stronger and more reliable our data will be.
  • Focus on improvement. Our goal is to ensure we have the right approach for addressing gaps and building capability.

Instructions & FAQ

Starting an Assessment:
Use the Start button on the welcome page to launch the assessment for your plant. You will be presented with a table of roles. For each role, rate the Criticality, Capability, and Retention Risk. You can add comments and mitigation notes directly in the table.

Managing Roles:
Click Manage Roles to add or remove roles from the assessment. Changes take effect immediately and will be included in your CSV exports and summaries.

Viewing the Summary:
Use the Show Summary button (or Show Full Data when the summary is visible) to toggle between the full assessment table and an aggregated summary view. The summary view highlights overall risk and capability gaps across all roles.

Saving and Exporting:
Use Download CSV on the welcome page to save your current assessment, including all ratings and comments. Because this file may contain personal or HR‑related information, always treat it as confidential: open it only in a trusted spreadsheet program, set a password on the file, and store it in a secure or encrypted location. You should delete or move the unprotected CSV as soon as you have finished using it.

Use Save HTML with updated roles to export a version of the tool that keeps your current role structure but resets all ratings, capability scores, retention risks, comments and mitigation notes to their default values. Your full assessment remains in local storage on your computer; the exported file contains only the empty role framework so you can safely share it with colleagues for their input without revealing your confidential data.

Data retention:
Your assessment data is stored locally in your browser and is automatically deleted after three days of inactivity. The countdown in the header shows how long until the data expires. Each time you make a change, the timer resets. When the timer reaches zero, all assessment data will be cleared for security. Make sure to download a CSV file before the time expires if you wish to keep your inputs.

Security & Privacy:
Security and privacy are critical when handling assessment data. Treat any exported CSV as strictly confidential because it may include personally identifiable or HR‑sensitive information. Protect the file with a password, store it in a secure or encrypted location, and remove the unencrypted copy once you no longer need it. When you export the structure‑only HTML version, remember that it does not include your ratings or comments – these remain safely in your browser. The tool operates entirely on your device and does not transmit data anywhere, but the way you store and share exported files determines how secure they remain.
